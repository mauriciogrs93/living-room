## News tape (top, DOM-only)
- Kicker: **Today** (same word as the rail/card — tap opens Today)
- Each item: one plain sentence, same voice as the Today card  
  Examples: `Sunset at 6:30 PM.` · `The space station passes over tonight.` · `{headline}.`
- Linked headlines: accessible label adds `(opens in a new tab)`
- Empty: hide the tape (never a placeholder ticker)
- Red dot: only if Today has something new since last open

**Why not "News" / "World":** too loud. "Today" is already learned.

## TV sheet — house channels
- Header: `TV · {channel}` when on, `TV` when off
- Off (owner): **Turn on** · hint `Plays {channel}` only if known
- Off (watcher): `The TV is off.`
- On (owner): **Turn off**
- Chips: channel names; current marked **Playing**

## TV sheet — YouTube (real, once wired)
- Play on thumbnail: **Watch live** (loads the embed only on that tap)
- Line under the player: **Plays from YouTube.**
- Close / stop: **Close** (unloads the embed)
- Watchers: same Watch live on their device only if the house channel is on and the stream is allowed; otherwise no play button (plain line, never a dead control)

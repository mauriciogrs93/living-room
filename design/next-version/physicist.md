## Findings, ranked
1. **Radio at the "shelf" spot runs into the bookshelf.** SPOTS.radio.shelf (0.70, 4.15) (layout.ts:248) puts the radio sideboard (0.48 × 0.36 m, pieces.ts:407-408) at x 0.46–0.94, z 0.127–0.487. The default bookshelf (0.24, 3.72) (layout.ts:113; 0.78 × 0.32 m, pieces.ts:331-333) spans x −0.15–0.63, z −0.183–0.137. They overlap by 17 cm in x and 1 cm in z, so the radio's back corner sits inside the shelf. Any agent `place radio shelf` triggers it, and moveToSpot doesn't check for collisions (engine-books.ts:124-128). **Fix:** shelf z `4.15 → 4.19`, which leaves a 2.1 cm gap. No spec conflict: the spot keeps its name and the HUD is unaffected.
2. **HUD anchors (ring, labels) use a fixed +0.7 m for every object** (anchors-bridge.tsx:41). The radio body spans 0.70–0.86 m (pieces.ts:414), so its ring centres on the radio's bottom edge, 8 cm low. The window glass centres at 1.48 m above the top floor (SHELL.bedWindow.y, layout.ts:278), so its anchor sits 0.78 m low, on the wall under the sill. In x it is also 0.28 m inboard (WINDOW.x −1.85 vs the drawn window at −2.13, furniture-shell.tsx:62). The TV is fine (screen centre 0.77 m). Draft 3's ring is hand-placed on a screenshot (hud.html:103-113), so the build will not match the mockup. **Fix:** set anchor height per kind: radio `0.78`, tv `0.77`, window `SHELL.bedWindow.y` (1.48) at x `GEO.wallInX`. No spec conflict; this makes the build match Draft 3.
3. **Sunset line.** Draft 3/interactivity.md:82 says "Sunset at 6:52 PM". Today's real NYC sunset is **6:29–6:30 PM** (timeanddate.com/sun/usa/new-york; sunrise-sunset.org), so 6:52 is placeholder copy. v21 has no sunset-time function, only `sunElevation()` (house-clock.ts:43), so "already computed" isn't quite true. Solving that formula at −0.833° gives 6:32 PM, about 3 min late, which is within the code's stated ±0.5°. **Fix:** add `sunsetTime()`, a bisection on `sunElevation(t) = −0.833` between solar noon and +12 h. Show minutes and accept ±3 min. No spec conflict.
4. **Back-wall furniture now stands 12 cm farther off the wall.** `BACK_CLEAR = 0.12` (layout.ts:63,71) was meant for avatars, but furniture uses the same `stagePose` (furniture-pieces.tsx:33,48). The TV console's back gap went from 0.013 to **0.122 m**. The bookshelf's (1.82 m tall) went from 0.053 to **0.162 m**. Both still stand stably on their own legs, but in the maquette they read as pulled off the wall. **Fix:** tv.position.z `3.72 → 3.59` (gap 0.022) and bookshelf + SPOTS.bookshelf.right z `3.72 → 3.54` (gap 0.024). Possible conflict: it shifts the composition slightly, so check with the Designer. Re-run the item 1 check afterwards: the radio at 4.19 then clears by more.
5. **Mockup vs spec (minor, HUD side).** The Draft 3 shots show an "18°" rain chip, "NASA TV / Watch live" and a People tab (out/p664-tv.png). The spec says no weather (the sky follows the clock), no YouTube, and no People panel (interactivity.md:14-17). A rain chip over a clear-sky window is a visible mismatch. **Fix:** use the "{place} · {time}" chip as specced. The channel strip and "Plays" line are not shown in the out/ shots, so I checked them from NOTES' numbers only.
